About Me

4.5+ Years of Building

Results-driven Mobile Application Developer with 4.5+ years of experience designing, building, and scaling Android (Java) and Flutter (Dart) applications.

I'm a mobile application developer with 4.5+ years of hands-on experience building products that span attendance tracking, sales operations, field-visit management, and content-consumption apps. My core strength is Android development in Java, where I've built and shipped several production applications from the ground up — and over the last year and a half, I've extended that into Flutter, giving me the ability to build cross-platform apps without duplicating engineering effort.

What I care about most isn't just writing code that works — it's writing code that keeps working. That's why a large part of my day-to-day involves Firebase Crashlytics and Performance Monitoring: catching instability before it becomes a support ticket, and understanding why an app slows down, not just that it did.

At Multiplier Brand Solution, I currently own and maintain 15+ live applications on the Google Play Store — which means I spend as much time thinking about release management, versioning, and backward compatibility as I do about new features. I've built RESTful API integrations that keep these apps in sync with backend systems reliably, and I lean on AI-assisted development tools to move faster without cutting corners on code quality.

I enjoy the moment a feature goes from a Jira ticket to something a real user taps on their phone — and I like being the person who's still around when something breaks in production, because that's usually where the real learning happens.
